# DATA PROTECTION POLICY (NDPA 2023 / GAID 2025)
## Grace of God Mission Asaba

**Document ID:** GGM-DPO-POL-001  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective Date:** 30 October 2026  
**Applicability:** All clergy, administrative staff, IT personnel, media unit members, and volunteers handling personal data.

---

### 1. PURPOSE AND OBJECTIVES
Grace of God Mission Asaba ("the Church") is dedicated to protecting the rights and fundamental freedoms of individuals in accordance with the **Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023** and the **General Application and Implementation Directive (GAID) 2025** issued by the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC).

This policy sets out mandatory standards for collecting, processing, storing, and disposing of personal information across all church departments.

---

### 2. CORE PRINCIPLES OF DATA PROCESSING (NDPA Section 24)
All personal data processed by the Church must be:
1. **Lawful, Fair, and Transparent:** Collected on a recognized lawful basis with clear notification to data subjects.
2. **Purpose Limited:** Collected for specified, explicit, and legitimate ministry purposes and not further processed in a manner incompatible with those purposes.
3. **Data Minimised:** Adequate, relevant, and strictly limited to what is necessary in relation to the stated purpose.
4. **Accurate and Current:** Reasonable steps must be taken to ensure that inaccurate data is promptly rectified or erased.
5. **Storage Limited:** Stored in a form that permits identification for no longer than is necessary for the stated purpose.
6. **Confidential and Secure:** Safeguarded against unauthorized access, alteration, accidental loss, damage, or destruction using technical and organizational measures.

---

### 3. LEGAL BASES FOR PROCESSING (NDPA Section 25)
Processing is only permitted when at least one of the following conditions is satisfied:
- **Consent:** The data subject has given explicit, informed, affirmative consent (e.g. prayer requests, testimonies, public inquiries).
- **Contractual / Stewardship Performance:** Processing is necessary for financial donation accounting or receipts.
- **Legal Obligation:** Compliance with mandatory statutory reporting or financial compliance under Nigerian law.
- **Vital Interests:** Protection of essential physical well-being in health emergencies.
- **Legitimate Ministry Interests:** Necessary for internal church governance, spiritual edification, and pastoral communication, provided the fundamental rights of the individual are not overridden.

---

### 4. CHILDREN'S DATA PROTECTION (NDPA Section 31)
- Under Nigerian law, any individual below the age of 18 is a child.
- The Church **shall not** collect or process the personal data of children through digital forms without verified parental or legal guardian consent.
- All digital forms include age-gating questions or explicit confirmation of parental consent.
- Images or recordings of children from church youth events will not be publicly identified with full contact information or addresses.

---

### 5. ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITIES
- **Church Leadership & Board of Trustees:** Overall oversight and accountability for institutional compliance.
- **Data Protection Officer (DPO) / Designated Lead:** Oversees compliance monitoring, audits, data subject requests, and serves as contact point for the NDPC.
- **Department Heads & Pastors:** Ensure that prayer records, counseling notes, and contact lists within their units are secured and not shared casually.
- **Technical & Media Team:** Implement encryption, authentication, access controls, backup integrity, and secure configuration across servers and databases.

---

### 6. DATA SECURITY AND ACCESS CONTROLS
- **Authentication:** All administrative users must access management portals using secure credentials and role-based permissions. Multi-factor authentication (MFA/2FA) is supported and strongly advised.
- **Encryption:** All web traffic must enforce TLS/HTTPS in transit. Sensitive data at rest must be secured with industry-standard cryptographic primitives.
- **Payment Security:** Financial payment transactions must be routed through PCI-DSS certified processors (Paystack). Raw debit card credentials and bank account passwords must **never** be collected or stored on Church servers.
- **Access Limitation:** Access to prayer petitions, donor personal records, and user management is granted strictly on a need-to-know basis.

---

### 7. DATA SUBJECT RIGHTS MANAGEMENT
Individuals whose data is held by the Church have statutory rights under Part VI of the NDPA (Sections 34–40). Requests must be handled without undue delay and within **30 days** of receipt:
- **Right to Access**
- **Right to Rectification**
- **Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten")**
- **Right to Object to Processing**
- **Right to Data Portability**
Requests should be submitted in writing to `info@ggmasaba.org` or `privacy@ggmasaba.org`.

---

### 8. INCIDENT AND BREACH MANAGEMENT (NDPA Section 40)
Any loss, exposure, or unauthorized access to personal data must be escalated immediately to the DPO. If a breach is assessed to present a risk to the rights and freedoms of individuals, the Church will notify the NDPC within **72 hours** of becoming aware of the incident, in line with statutory requirements.

---

### 9. REVIEW AND REVISION
This policy shall be formally reviewed on an annual basis or upon the enactment of updated directives by the NDPC.
