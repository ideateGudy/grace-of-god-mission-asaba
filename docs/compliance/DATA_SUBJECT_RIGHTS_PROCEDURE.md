# DATA SUBJECT RIGHTS PROCEDURE (NDPA 2023)
## Grace of God Mission Asaba

**Document ID:** GGM-DPO-SOP-005  
**Effective Date:** 30 October 2026  
**Governing Standard:** NDPA 2023 Sections 34–40, GAID 2025

---

### 1. OVERVIEW
Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023, data subjects possess fundamental rights concerning their personal information. This procedure establishes the operational steps Grace of God Mission Asaba follows to receive, verify, process, and fulfill data subject requests in a timely, transparent, and compliant manner.

---

### 2. SUMMARY OF STATUTORY RIGHTS

1. **Right of Access (NDPA s.34):** The right to obtain confirmation as to whether personal data is being processed, and to receive a clear, free copy of the personal data held.
2. **Right to Rectification (NDPA s.35):** The right to require correction of inaccurate, out-of-date, incomplete, or misleading data without undue delay.
3. **Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten", NDPA s.36):** The right to request permanent deletion of personal data when:
   - The data is no longer necessary in relation to the purposes for which it was collected;
   - The data subject withdraws consent and no other lawful basis exists;
   - The personal data has been unlawfully processed.
   *(Note: Erasure cannot override statutory financial/tax retention obligations).*
4. **Right to Restrict Processing (NDPA s.37):** The right to request temporary suspension of processing while accuracy or objections are verified.
5. **Right to Data Portability (NDPA s.38):** The right to receive personal data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format (e.g., CSV or JSON).
6. **Right to Object (NDPA s.39):** The right to object at any time to processing based on legitimate interests or direct communications.
7. **Right to Withdraw Consent (NDPA s.25(2)):** The right to withdraw consent at any time as easily as it was given.

### 4. STEP-BY-STEP FULFILLMENT WORKFLOW

#### Step 1: Logging and Acknowledgment (Day 1–3)
- Upon receipt, the request is logged in the internal **Data Subject Rights Register**.
- An acknowledgment email is issued to the requester within **72 hours** confirming receipt.

#### Step 2: Identity Verification (Day 3–5)
- To prevent unauthorized disclosure of personal data to impersonators, the DPO or Church Administrator verifies the requester's identity by:
  - Matching the request email with the email recorded in the relevant inquiry, donation, or testimony record; OR
  - Requesting verification of a recent transaction reference (for financial donors); OR
  - Viewing a valid government ID (where high-sensitivity personal data is involved).

#### Step 3: Assessment and Evaluation (Day 5–10)
- Determine whether the request is valid and whether any legal exemptions apply:
  - *Financial Records:* Cannot be immediately erased if within the statutory 6-year financial audit window under Nigerian law.
  - *Public Testimonies:* Can be immediately un-published or pseudonymized upon request.
  - *Contact Inquiries & Prayer Requests:* Can be permanently erased from database tables.

#### Step 4: Technical Execution (Day 10–20)
- **For Erasure:** Database administrator executes targeted deletion of matching records from `ContactMessage`, `PrayerRequest`, or `Testimony`.
- **For Access / Portability:** System administrator extracts relevant data in structured CSV or JSON format.
- **For Rectification:** Database record updated with verified accurate details.

#### Step 5: Formal Response & Closure (Within 30 Days)
- A formal written response is sent to the requester detailing the actions taken.
- If a request is declined in whole or part pursuant to a statutory exemption, the response must state the specific legal reason and inform the requester of their right to lodge a complaint with the **Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC)**.
